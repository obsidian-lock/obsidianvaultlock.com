What it does

One job, done quietly.

A passphrase, a lock that takes care of itself, and one folder that stays out of sight. That is the whole plugin.

Your passphrase is the key

Obsidian opens locked and stays locked until you type your passphrase. Wrong guesses are slowed down more and more, so nobody can rush through them.

Locks by itself

Five quiet minutes and the screen locks. It can also lock the moment you switch to another app, and Lock vault now locks it on the spot.

A real lock screen

The whole window goes behind the lock, pop-out windows included. Keyboard shortcuts and the command palette stop working, Settings closes, and the window title shows only "Obsidian".

A folder that disappears

Notes in your Private folder leave Obsidian while it is locked: not in the file list, not in search, not in the graph. Unlock, and they are back.

Nothing slips through

Private notes that are open on screen are closed before the lock comes down. If Obsidian crashes or is force-closed, it starts locked the next time.

Nothing to configure

Choose a passphrase and you are done. If you like, change the idle time, lock when switching apps, or rename the private folder. That is all there is.

For Obsidian 1.5 or newer on Windows, macOS and Linux. Not for phones and tablets.

Get it

Three steps. Then it just works.

Vault Lock arrives through BRAT, a small helper that adds plugins to Obsidian. No account, no sign-up.

  1. 1

    Get Obsidian

    Obsidian is the free notes app that Vault Lock works inside. Download it, open it and create a vault, which is simply the folder that will hold your notes. Already using Obsidian? Skip to step 2.

    Download Obsidian
  2. 2

    Add Vault Lock

    Press the button. Obsidian opens and BRAT adds Vault Lock. If it is not switched on afterwards, turn it on under Settings → Community plugins.

    Install with BRAT
    No BRAT yet? Add it first, it takes a minute.
    1. In Obsidian open Settings → Community plugins and press Turn on community plugins.
    2. Press Browse, search for BRAT, then Install and Enable.
    3. Come back here and press Install with BRAT. Or, in BRAT's own settings, choose Add beta plugin, paste this and press Add:
    obsidian-lock/vault-lock
  3. 3

    Choose your passphrase

    The first time Vault Lock starts, it asks you for a passphrase. Pick one you will remember. From then on, it is what unlocks your notes.

The button did nothing?
  • Your browser may ask whether to open Obsidian. Allow it.
  • Obsidian must be installed and open. The button hands over to Obsidian, and nothing else can answer it.
  • BRAT must be installed and enabled first. Step 2 shows how.
  • Vault Lock runs on computers only. Obsidian on phones and tablets is not supported.

Good to know

Questions people ask.

Does it encrypt my files?

No. Vault Lock is a lock screen: it keeps your notes off the screen and your private folder out of Obsidian while locked. The files themselves stay as they are. If you worry about a lost or stolen computer, also turn on your system's disk encryption: BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on Mac.

Can I change the passphrase?

Yes. While unlocked, run Set or change passphrase from the command palette. It asks for the current one first.

Will it get in my way?

Only when it is time to lock. While you are writing, nothing changes. The lock screen appears when Obsidian starts, after five quiet minutes, or whenever you ask for it.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Vault Lock is for Obsidian on a computer: Windows, macOS or Linux.
