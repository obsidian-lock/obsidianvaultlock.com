Is it for me?

Yes, if you keep notes on a computer that other people sometimes see: family at home, colleagues at work, anyone walking past. New to Obsidian? The steps below start there.

What it does

Three things. Nothing else.

Locks on its own

When Obsidian starts. After five minutes without you. The moment you switch to another window, if you turn that on. Or right now, with the command Lock vault now.

Covers the whole window

While locked, the passphrase screen covers everything and pop-out windows go blank. Keyboard shortcuts stop working, and the window title says only "Obsidian".

Hides one folder

A folder named Private at the top of your vault, the folder where Obsidian keeps your notes, is gone while locked: not in the file list, not in search. Unlock and it is back.

Vault Lock locks the screen. It does not encrypt your files.

The Private folder

There while you work.
Gone while you are away.

Any open note from Private is closed first, then the folder disappears. Unlock, and it is back where it was.

Get it

Four steps. The last one is choosing a passphrase.

Already have Obsidian? Start at step 2. Already have BRAT? Step 3.

  1. 1

    Get Obsidian

    Obsidian is a free app for writing notes. It keeps them as ordinary files on your computer. Download it, install it, then open it and create a vault for your notes.

    Download Obsidian
  2. 2

    Install BRAT

    Obsidian can be extended with plugins: small add-ons made by other people. Vault Lock is not in Obsidian's built-in plugin list, so another plugin, BRAT, installs it.

    1. In Obsidian, open Settings, then Community plugins.
    2. Press Turn on community plugins. Obsidian calls the off state Restricted mode.
    3. Choose Browse, search for BRAT, press Install, then Enable.
  3. 3

    Install Vault Lock

    Press the button. Obsidian opens and BRAT adds Vault Lock.

    Install Vault Lock

    Then go back to Settings, Community plugins, and switch Vault Lock on if it is not on already.

    Button did nothing?
    • Your browser may ask whether to open Obsidian. Say yes.
    • Obsidian has to be installed and open. Only Obsidian can answer the button.
    • BRAT has to be installed and enabled. See step 2.
    • It does not work on phones or tablets.
    • Or add it by hand: in BRAT's settings choose Add beta plugin, paste the name below and press Add.
    obsidian-lock/vault-lock
  4. 4

    Choose a passphrase

    Vault Lock asks for one the first time it starts. Pick one you will remember. That is the whole setup.

Rather not use BRAT? Install by hand

From the latest release download main.js, manifest.json and styles.css into <your vault>/.obsidian/plugins/vault-lock/. Reload Obsidian and enable Vault Lock under Community plugins. Those three files are the whole plugin.

After that

What happens next

  • Vault Lock asks for your passphrase when Obsidian starts and after five quiet minutes.
  • Make a folder named Private at the top of your vault. Whatever you put in it is hidden while locked.
  • Settings, if you want them: the idle minutes, lock when the window loses focus, the private folder's name, your passphrase. Nothing else.
  • Two commands: Lock vault now and Set or change passphrase.
Vault Lock Free and open source: the code is public on GitHub under the MIT licence. Needs Obsidian 1.5 or newer.
